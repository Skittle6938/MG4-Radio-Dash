**Radio Dash 1.6.3 beta 1**

- **MG4Dash no longer switches back to DAB+ while you listen to FM** ([MG4Dash #80](https://github.com/Skittle6938/MG4Dash/issues/80)): the DAB+ tuner keeps receiving in the background during FM, and its info put DAB+ back on the MG4Dash music card while the sound stayed on FM. It now only reaches MG4Dash when the radio is on DAB+.
- **DAB+ picture kept**: on a station that sends no logo, the player and MG4Dash now keep its last slideshow picture, like the station list, instead of showing nothing after 5 minutes.
- **RDS accents shown**: accented letters (é, è, à, ç…) in the radio text and station names were invisible. They now show in Radio Dash and in MG4Dash.

Not yet checked on the car. Install from MG4Dash (Settings > System > Update > Update Radio Dash, with beta versions on), or like before (`adb push` + `pm install -r -f`, or USB stick), then **reboot the head unit** (hold Home about 20 s): the steering wheel buttons don't reach the radio until you do.
